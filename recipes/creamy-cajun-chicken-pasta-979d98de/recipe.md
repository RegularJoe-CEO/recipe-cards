**Servings:** 6

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add 1 Tbsp salt.
2. Cook pasta according to package instructions until al dente.
3. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water then drain, cover, and keep warm.
4. Beat the chicken breasts with a meat mallet so that they are even in thickness and season all over with 1 Tbsp Cajun seasoning.
5. Heat 2 tsp oil in a large non-reactive skillet over medium-high heat.
6. Once hot, add the chicken and sear on both sides.
7. Turn heat down to low and sauté until cooked through and 165°F on a thermometer.
8. Transfer to a cutting board, slice into thin strips, and cover to keep warm.
9. Sauté the butter and garlic in the same skillet over medium heat for 30-60 seconds or until the garlic is fragrant.
10. Add the diced tomatoes and sauté another 2 minutes.
11. Add in the heavy whipping cream, add the remaining 1/2 to 1 Tbsp Cajun seasoning, to taste, and parmesan cheese, and bring to a simmer.
12. Season to taste if needed.
13. Add the sliced chicken and cooked pasta to the sauce and toss until combined and pasta and chicken are warmed through.
14. Add warm reserved pasta water if desired to thin the sauce.
15. Serve with freshly grated parmesan and chopped parsley.
