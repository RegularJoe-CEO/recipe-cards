# Masa Biscuits

**Servings:** 12  
**Prep:** Heat your oven to 425 degrees F.

## Method

1. *(prep)* Heat your oven to 425 degrees F.
2. In a medium mixing bowl, use a whisk to combine the dry ingredients: flour, masa harina, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. Add the buttermilk and stir to combine.
4. Fold in the butter cubes.
5. Pat the dough into a 1-inch-thick rectangle. Use a sharp knife to cut the dough into 12 equally sized square biscuits. Press the knife straight down and don't drag it; dragging will prevent the biscuits from rising as high as they can. Place the squares on a parchment-lined sheet pan.
6. Bake for 15 minutes. The biscuits will be pretty blond when they are ready, so don't overcook them in the name of getting a golden crust. Serve warm.
