## Method

1. Using bones, cartilage, uncooked gizzards, liver, turkey neck, turkey wing tips and any scraps from the skin, add to a large stock pot and cover with enough water to allow 2 or 3 inches of water on top.
2. Simmer the broth on medium-low heat, checking every 30-40 minutes and stirring, for 2-4 hours. Add water as needed the first hour, and then let the broth condense for use in the gumbo.
3. Remove the bones and neck after cooling and strain the broth with a colander, removing remaining pieces of turkey bones and scrapings.
4. Measure equal parts oil and all-purpose flour.
5. Place a cast iron skillet over medium to medium-low heat and heat the oil.
6. Whisk or sprinkle the flour into the oil until smooth.
7. Stir the oil and flour frequently until small bubbles appear. Do not fry the flour or let it boil.
8. Stir or whisk continuously, until the color is similar to a penny, and continue scraping the bottom and corners of the pan so it does not scorch or burn.
9. Once the roux is made, remove from heat and set aside.
10. Chop 2-3 ribs of celery, including leafy greens. Chop 1 medium or medium-large yellow onion.
11. Shred or chop leftover turkey meat into spoon-size chunks.
12. Sauté the onions and celery with oil on medium-high heat until the onions are translucent and the celery is tender. Be careful not to brown the onions.
13. Combine the onion, celery, roux and broth in a large stockpot and heat on medium until thoroughly blended. (Add water if needed)
14. Add the turkey meat and ensure the liquid broth is covering the meat with 1-2 inches on top. (Add water if needed)
15. Simmer the gumbo on low heat for 1 hour and taste test. Add salt and pepper as needed. Also add McIlhenny Tabasco sauce or Louisiana hot sauce to taste.
16. Return to low heat, simmering for at least 1 additional hour.
17. Meanwhile, stew young tender okra (tops discarded, sliced 1/2 inch to 1 inch) with tomatoes (fresh and crushed, or canned) in a separate large pot while the gumbo is simmering.
18. Serve the gumbo over rice with filé or okra with stewed tomatoes or green onions, and French baguette with butter.

## Notes

Missy uses EVOO for the oil.
