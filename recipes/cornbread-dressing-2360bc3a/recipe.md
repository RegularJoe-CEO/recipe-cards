## Method

1. Make the cornbread according to the package directions.
2. Cook the chicken in the chicken broth.
3. Debone the chicken and save all the broth.
4. Sauté the onions, celery, apple and sweet corn (optional) in the stick of butter.
5. Crumble the cornbread and biscuits into a big pot.
6. Mix in the onion mixture.
7. Pour all the chicken broth into the pot and add the deboned chicken.
8. Combine everything and stir it up real good until smooth.
9. Add the eggs to the mixture one at a time and mix well.
10. Stir in salt, pepper and sage to taste.
11. Bake at 325°F until done, about 30-45 minutes.
