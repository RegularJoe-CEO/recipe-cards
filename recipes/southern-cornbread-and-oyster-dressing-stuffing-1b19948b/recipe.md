# Southern Cornbread and Oyster Dressing (Stuffing)

**Servings:** 8 to 10 side servings  
**Prep:** When ready to bake dressing, preheat oven to 350 degrees F.  
**Prep:** Butter a 9 x 13-inch pan or spray with non-stick spray.

## Method

1. Toast the bread in a toaster or in the oven.
2. Remove and set aside to cool.
3. Make a pan of cornbread, remove and set aside uncovered to cool completely.
4. You want the breads to be very dry.
5. *(prep)* When ready to bake dressing, preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
6. *(prep)* Butter a 9 x 13-inch pan or spray with non-stick spray.
7. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat.
8. Saute the onion, celery and bell pepper.
9. Add the salt, Creole or Cajun seasoning, pepper, sage or Bell's seasoning and poultry seasoning.
10. Continue cooking and stirring for 2 minutes.
11. Set aside to cool.
12. Crumble the cornbread into a large bowl.
13. Add the bread and toss.
14. Add the sauteed veggies, scraping out all of the butter; stir.
15. Pour in 2 cups of the broth and toss.
16. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
17. Add the beaten eggs; stir.
18. Using a pair of kitchen shears, roughly chop the oysters.
19. Add the oysters, with their juices, to the cornbread mixture.
20. This is where you fix the consistency.
21. If you want a fluffy stuffing, you'll use less stock.
22. If you prefer a wetter stuffing, add additional broth as needed to reach desired consistency, taking care not to get too soupy!
23. Lightly spoon into the prepared casserole dish, but do not pack in.
24. Bake at 350 degrees F for 45 minutes.

## Notes

This can be done in advance. For a moister stuffing, baked covered. For a drier stuffing with a crunchy top, bake uncovered.
