## Method

1. Aromatics Heat a heavy based skillet over high heat (no oil) until smoking.
2. Place onion and ginger in pan cut side down.
3. Cook for a few minutes until it's charred, then turn.
4. Remove and set aside.
5. Toast Spices lightly in a dry skillet over medium high heat for 3 minutes.&nbsp; Remove impurities: Rinse bones &amp; brisket then cover with water in large stock pot.
6. Boil for 5 minutes, then drain.
7. Rinse each bone and brisket under tap water.
8. Broth: Wipe pot clean, bring 3.5 litres / 3.75 quarts water to boil.
9. Add bones and brisket, onion, ginger, Spices Add onion, ginger, Spices, sugar and salt - water should just barely cover everything.
10. Cover with lid, simmer 3 hours.
11. Remove brisket (should be fall-apart tender), cool then refrigerate for later.
12. Simmer remaining soup UNCOVERED for 40 minutes.
13. Strain broth into another pot, discard bones and spices.
14. Should be about 2.5 litres / 2.65 quarts (10 cups), if loads more, reduce.
15. Add fish sauce, adjust salt and sugar if needed.
16. Broth should be beefy, fragrant with spices, savoury and barely sweet.
17. Assemble: Prepare rice noodles per packet, just prior to serving.
18. Place noodles in bowl.
19. Top with raw beef and brisket.
20. Ladle over about 400 / 14 oz hot broth - will cook beef to medium rare.
21. Serve with Toppings on the side!
